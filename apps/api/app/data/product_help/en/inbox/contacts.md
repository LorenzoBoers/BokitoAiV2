# How Contacts works

Everyone who writes in — email, chat or another channel — lands here so the team can recognize, approve and group them.

## People and companies

Switch between people and companies. Link contacts to a company so related threads stay grouped. Use Link contacts if older threads never received a company.

## Approve and block

New addresses can wait for approval. Approved contacts stay in the book. Blocked senders drop out of normal handling.

## Jump back to the work

From a contact you open their threads in Communication. The contact record is the person; the inbox is the work.

## What to do next

Connect email or install the website widget so contacts start appearing automatically.
