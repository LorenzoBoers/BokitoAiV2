# Invite the team

Members work conversations. Owners and admins also change settings and review Govern drafts. Open **Settings**, then **Members**.

## Invite someone

![Members settings](/api/docs/assets/members/invite.png)
*Owners and admins invite the team here.*

1. Open **Settings**, then **Members**.
2. Under **Invite a teammate**, enter a full email (Press Enter to send) and set **Role**: **Admin** or **Member**. A short line under the form explains what that role can do. The table **What each role can do** shows invite, change agents, and handle conversations.
3. Choose **Invite**. They join after they accept. If this server cannot send mail, the page says invite emails are not sent yet — use **Copy invite link** and share it yourself. An empty list offers **Invite a member**.

## Follow a pending invite

1. Switch the list with **All**, **Active** or **Pending**. Search the table when it is long.
2. On **Pending**, choose **Resend invite email** if they never got the mail. **Copy invite link** if you need to send it another way.
3. **Revoke invite** when that address should not join.

## Change a role or remove access

1. Switch to **Active** and open the member in the list.
2. Change the role if their job changed. Making someone an **Owner** asks for confirmation — they get billing and who-can-join control. Only the current **Owner** can promote, demote, or remove an owner; admins manage admins and members only.
3. **Remove member** when they should no longer see workspace mail or settings.

To delete the whole workspace, an owner opens **Settings**, then **General**, and uses **Delete workspace**. Platform support can also delete a tenant from **Ops**.

Members answer threads. They do not connect mailboxes or change autonomy. They can lower AI handling on a conversation, contact or channel, but only an owner or admin can raise it to Autonomous. See [Govern](/docs/govern/govern) for who may accept platform changes.

## Personal settings stay personal

Each person has **Profile** and **Notifications**. Those screens are theirs. On Profile they set **Start page** (**Messages** or **Overview**), **Appearance**, language, and a personal **Email signature** used when a reply is sent as them. On Notifications they choose **Notify me about** rows (decisions, mentions, handoffs, budget alerts) with **In-app**, **Email**, **Push** and optional **Slack**. See [Decisions](/docs/ai/decisions) for how notification clicks land on waiting cards. They do not change how the team answers customers. See [Agents](/docs/ai/agents) for company agents, and [Messages](/docs/inbox/communication) for the shared inbox.
