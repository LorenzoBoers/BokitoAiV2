# Product tour

Bokito has one sidebar and a handful of areas. Each answers a different question.

## Cockpit - what needs me today?

The Cockpit is the daily start screen: open threads that need attention, recent agent activity and pending approvals. Scan it in the morning and after lunch instead of keeping every page open.

## Communication - the conversations

Every customer conversation across all channels, plus internal threads with your agents. Threads carry the full context: contact, history, AI drafts and inline decision requests. This is where most of the day happens.

## Agenda - the schedule

Scheduled agent work and automations: recurring runs, follow-ups, triggers. If something should happen every day at nine, it lives here.

## Contacts - the people

Everyone you talk to, with conversation history and notes. Opens from any thread by clicking the contact.

## Projects - the longer work

Work that spans days or weeks, shared between people and agents: a task list with context that agents can pick up from.

## Knowledge - what agents know

The documents agents answer from, and customer-facing help articles you publish. Better knowledge means better drafts.

## Agents - the AI workforce

Each agent's role, instructions, model and tools, plus its work logs. Come here to add an agent or understand why one behaved the way it did.

## Settings - the control room

Channels, members, models, integrations, the widget and Govern (autonomy, approvals, audit, API tokens). Owners and admins spend time here; members rarely need it.

## Two shortcuts worth learning

The command palette (`Ctrl+K` or `Cmd+K`) jumps anywhere and runs common actions. The help menu in the top bar opens these articles from any page.
