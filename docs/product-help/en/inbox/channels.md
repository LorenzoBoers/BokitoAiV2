# Connect channels

Channels are how customers reach the workspace. Connect a mailbox first. Website chat, WhatsApp and Slack follow when you need them.

## Connect a mailbox

Open Channel settings and start Gmail or Outlook. After the account is connected, inbound mail becomes threads in Communication. Signatures, folders and routing rules live on the same page.

## Route and sign

Routing rules assign or label inbound mail. Saved replies and signatures keep outbound mail consistent. Inbox AI modes (when to draft, when to stay quiet) live under Inbox AI settings.

## Other channels

Website chat is the Bokito widget. WhatsApp and Slack appear as queues after they are connected from Integrations. Each connected channel gets its own view in Communication.

## What to do next

Connect one mailbox, then open Communication and wait for the first thread. Install the widget when you want website visitors in the same hub.
