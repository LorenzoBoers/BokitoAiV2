# How Communication works

Customer messages, agent chats and internal threads share one inbox. Connect channels, then reply yourself or let AI draft answers.

## Work the Open queue

Open is customer work that still needs you. More hides Snoozed, Closed and Spam. Pin important threads, assign owners, and snooze what can wait.

## Connect channels

Email, website chat, WhatsApp and Slack each get a queue when connected. Channel settings and Inbox AI modes live under Settings.

## Decide in the thread

Mention an agent or start a chat from New chat. Agent runs and decision cards appear inline. Approve, edit or reject before anything goes out to a customer.

## Keep runs out of Open

Scheduled scans and waiting decisions live under Agent runs, not in Open. Cockpit “Awaiting decision” opens the same list.

## What to do next

Connect a mailbox on Channel settings, install the website widget, or open Contacts to see who is writing in.
