# How Cockpit works

Cockpit is the pulse of the workspace. Scan health first, then jump into the thread or agent that needs you.

## Scan the day on Overview

Cards show open work, pending decisions, agent runs and custom KPIs. Use this tab to see the day at a glance. Attention items deep-link into Communication or Agenda so you do not hunt for the source.

## Audit what happened on Activity

Activity is a combined timeline of agent runs and human actions (status changes, assignments, invites). Filter by agents or people when you need to reconstruct what happened.

## Watch spend on Usage

Usage shows model spend and volume against the workspace budget. Caps live here so operators can raise or pause spend without leaving Cockpit.

## What to do next

Open Communication for customer work that still needs a person. Open Agenda for scheduled wakes. If the workspace is still empty, follow the setup guide.
