# Privacy statement (product / website)

**Status:** `draft` — requires counsel review.  
**Version:** 0.1 · **Last updated:** 2026-09-01

## Who we are

Bokito provides an AI operations platform for SMBs. Contact for privacy: privacy@bokito.ai (replace with the official address once counsel-approved).

## Roles

- When you use Bokito for your organisation’s mail, contacts, and agents, **you are typically the controller**; Bokito is the **processor**.
- For our own website accounts, billing, and marketing, Bokito is the **controller**.

## What we process (as processor)

On your instructions: mailbox content, contacts, calendar events, agent runs, decisions, feedback, and integration metadata. See the DPA and sub-processors list.

## What we process (as controller)

Account email, name, authentication data, workspace membership, support tickets, usage/billing metadata, and security logs.

## Purposes and legal bases (controller)

- Contract performance: provide the service, authenticate users.
- Legitimate interests: security, abuse prevention, product improvement (aggregated/non-customer-content where possible).
- Consent: where required (e.g. optional marketing).

## Retention

Default message and calendar retention is 365 days (configurable by workspace owners). Account data is kept for the life of the account; anonymisation on account delete. See workspace Trust settings.

## Sharing

Sub-processors (LLM providers, hosting, email delivery, error monitoring, Google/Microsoft for OAuth). No sale of personal data.

## International transfers

Documented in SUBPROCESSORS with SCCs / DPF / EU hosting where applicable.

## Rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and complaint to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Workspace owners can use Trust tools for subject export/erase assistance.

## AI

Customer-facing chat may involve AI systems. Operators use Govern for human oversight. See AI-ACT-CLASSIFICATION.md.

## Changes

Material changes will be announced in-product or by email.
